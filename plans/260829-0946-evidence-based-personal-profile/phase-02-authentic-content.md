# Phase 2: Authentic content

## Outcome

Create a publishable evidence pack: real portrait, three featured narratives, approved Operating Atlas nodes, working principles, current focus, and confirmed connection paths.

## Requirements

- Keep three featured narratives for comprehension: one Build, one Contribution/Operate, and one Codify.
- Include every verified operational project approved for publication as a smaller Operating Atlas evidence node.
- Each featured narrative has role, problem, constraint, decisions, proof, result, and lesson.
- Result language distinguishes observed outcome from aspiration.
- Use approved screenshots, diagrams, documents, links, or anonymized artifacts.
- No placeholder project, metric, testimonial, company, or image may reach implementation.
- Current focus must be current and time-bounded; omit it if unavailable.

## Candidate project families

- AI/developer tooling: `myskills`, `devin-switcher`, `tools-managers`, agent/runtime work
- Operational systems: CPPAI/Lark, Peaceful Life, recruitment or another approved system
- Codified knowledge: ebook/handbook, skills, frameworks, training or research workflows
- Optional personal experiment: Langautrip, beautiFULLshot, or another approved project

Candidate names are research inputs, not publication approval.

## Steps
1. Use the [project portfolio audit](./research/project-portfolio-audit.md) to build a publication matrix.
2. Mark every candidate public, anonymized, or private.
3. Select three featured narratives without hiding other approved operational evidence.
4. Define the role, proof, and destination for every approved Operating Atlas node.
5. Gather approved media and links.
6. Draft Vietnamese case narratives in first-person, plain language.
7. Produce equivalent English copy after Vietnamese approval.
8. Draft About, Practice, Current Focus, and Contact content.
9. Run factual and privacy review.

## Deliverables

- `research/project-publication-matrix.md`
- approved asset inventory
- Vietnamese/English content pack
- three complete case-study briefs

## Success criteria

- [ ] Three featured narratives span Build, Contribution/Operate, and Codify.
- [ ] Every verified operational project is either represented in the Atlas or explicitly excluded by privacy/evidence review.
- [ ] Every claim has a publishable source or direct user confirmation.
- [ ] Every image has ownership/permission and useful alt text.
- [ ] Copy sounds like an experienced practitioner, not generic marketing.
- [ ] User approves content before visual prototyping.
